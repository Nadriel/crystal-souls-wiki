## Birth of Chaos
At the beginning there was everything, time, space, energy, entropy, power, magic, faith, life and death and it was all together, all at once, everywhere and nowhere forever and never. And for a long time, all of that was happening at the same time, due to luck, chance, or a greater will, something changed. And all of it condensed into a singular entity of everything, an entity with a will and a consciousness. And thus [[Chaos]] was born.

## Birth of Law
After [[Chaos]] willed itself into existence, it tried to understand everything. However, due to its own nature, that was impossible, so, in its hubris, it decided to create [[Law]] as a way to understand itself by examining its opposite. And thus [[Law]] was born, as the same, but also the opposite of [[Chaos]]. 

## The Titans And the Universe
As Law and Chaos faced each other, they instantly knew that each one defined the other by existence and also absence. That is the absurdity of such concepts and their personifications did not miss that. Instantly the two beings began their insane, eternal dance, whirling around each other, challenging, fighting, loving, creating and destroying, always together but never one. And from their dance, they birthed the **Concepts**, by combining chance and randomness of Chaos with form and function of Law. And, as they had done with themselves, they blessed the **Concepts** with consciousness, thus birthing the [[Titans]]. The [[Titans]] became the first beings, the first ideas of everything mortals would later come to recognise as the [[Universe]]. As the Titans were created, Law and Chaos looked upon them, huge and bright and full of power and suddenly, they understood. Not everything, mind you, but certainly some of everything. And for the first time, the [[Entities]] found some peace. And so, they decided to scatter their children all across the emptiness of space and let them live, and create and thrive, hoping that through their quest to find each other again, they would come to understand. 

Each titan then, found itself alone in the depths of space. Gifted with unimaginable power to create and destroy, each representing ideas and concepts in an immense scale, and with an unstoppable drive to find each other again, they began creating and walking, thinking and imagining. And thus they created the Universe as we know it.
## Our Planet
Our planet was birthed by the Titan [[ALea'Therthos]] as we call him in the Common tongue, a direct translation of "Joka Rakastaa" or "The One who Loves" in Giant speech. ALea'Therthos is the Titan of Light, Love, Magic, Song and Dance, and as he began his journey, he wanted to create beings like him, so that he would share the wonders of his mind. Thus, he created our system of planets, including our planet, [[]] and set the  spinning around his head. Upon our planet, he set it the [[Giants]], his precious sons and daughters. With them, he shared his Light, by becoming the Sun, his constant light carrying Magic to his Sons and Daughters. However, even though ALea'Therthos is a Titan who aligns himself more with his parent Law, the touch of Chaos was too much for his creations. The Giants, almost as soon as they were created, started fighting with each other. And from their fighting came the mountains and the plains, the oceans and the rivers and seas. And finally after eons of fighting, they grew tired and settled to sleep. And at that moment, ALea'Therthos stopped walking, for the first time ever and closed his eyes and wept. His tears fell upon the slumbering giants and the planets flooded from the tears of our Titan Sun and thus began the time of the [[Cataclysm]].  ALea'Therthos wept for so long for his lost sons and daughters and the hate and pain they had caused each other, that finally, he closed his eyes and slept. As he slept, his light dimmed and the Planets were thrust in darkness and cold. And the tears he had shed and had flooded our planet froze and thus began our Planet's [[Ice Age]].  
And during his sleep, he dreamt. He dreamt of good times and bad, of things that were and weren't. And from his dreams and nightmares, the [[Gods]] were born! The populated our Planet, roaming the endless tundras and icy wastes, with the ideas of the slumbering Titan for a better and more interesting and loving world, readying it for when the Titan ALea'Therthos would awake again. As the Gods readied the Planet for the Titan's reawakening, they started experimenting with creation themselves. Interacting with the Icy Planet, they birthed from their own imaginations the first [[Mortals]] and let them explore the world, while they sat back and enjoyed the freedom and joy of creation and sometimes destruction. However, the first [[Mortals]] having being birthed from [[Gods]], did not possess the reason and intelligence that we have come to expect from ourselves today. For the [[Gods]] at that time were still young and not wise, as they were simply made from the Titan's dreams and nightmares. Back then, civilization was not even a concept our [[Gods]] could fathom and we [[Mortals]] spent our times wandering the planet, much like our Giant cousins before us, fighting amongst ourselves for survival, dominion and other petty reasons. For hundreds of years ALea'Therthos slept, and the Gods played with us and with each other, learning in the process, sometimes even finding the sleeping [[Giants]], their brothers and sisters and teasing them, sometimes waking them to fight, or love or kill and other times leaving them alone. 

That is until the [[Sun's Awakening]], when our Titan Father ALea'Therthos, awoke from his slumber finally! That is what we now call our Year 0, the birth of Civilization and the beginning of the [[Age of Mortals]].
