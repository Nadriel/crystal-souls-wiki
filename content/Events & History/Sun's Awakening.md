## Overview
This is the time when the Titan of our Sun, [[ALea'Therthos]] awoke from his slumber and ended the [[Ice Age]]. 

As his eyes first opened and the light fell upon our Planet, the Ice began to melt and the wonder that the [[Gods]] had created revealed itself to [[ALea'Therthos]]. Surprised by what his imagination had birthed during his slumber, the Titan rejoiced at the wonderful, multicoloured planet. Where rock and lava and chaos thrived, while the [[Giants]] ruled our planet, now among side the barren plains and the mountains and oceans and rivers, there were thriving forests, flowered fields, wheat covered plains and lush jungles. And there, among the trees and plains and mountains, he saw the [[Gods]] and the [[Mortals]] : Animals, fish, birds and insects and things even smaller than that. And his surprise turned to joy, as he saw a chance to fix the mistake he had made with his first children. He decided to give a gift to the Gods and Mortals.
