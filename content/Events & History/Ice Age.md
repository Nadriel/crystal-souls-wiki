## Overview
This is the time when the Titan of our Sun, [[ALea'Therthos]] slept after weeping for three hundred years. As he closed his eyes, the planet which was flooded from the [[Cataclysm]] was frozen over. 

During this time, from the Titan's dreams and nightmares, the [[Gods]] were born and populated the planet. As the [[Gods]] explored the planet, they created the [[Mortals]].
