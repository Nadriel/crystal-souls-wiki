## Background
She was born in the town of [[Dulcherin]], that is located in [[GlimmerWoods]].

Dulcherin was a town build mainly in a clearing inside the woods. The remote parts of it were inside the forest. Many homes were built by combining parts of the forest with the buildings (similar to how the hobbits dug and incorporated their homes in the earth).
  
Willow’s mother was from a town close to Dulcherin who, after an abusive marriage, sought refuge in Dulcherin. She gave birth to her daughter there but lost her life to an illness before Willow turned one.

Willow doesn’t really remember her mom.

She grew up in one of the town’s orphanage (the one closest to the edge of the deep forest). Because most of the residents of Dulcherin were people living in harmony with nature they were generally kind people (if a little strange)  
All the children grew up together, without care about one’s family or financial situation or lineage. They learned about the forest and the seasons and the gifts [[Basalt]] and [[Gliss]] bestowed upon them with their sacrifice. And they were taught to respect and protect the forest and the mountain and the creeks.  
And then the dwarfs came from over the sea.

They burned the forest and blew up the mountain and run the creeks dry.  Some resides of  Dulcherin stayed behind to fight and take the forest back, but Willow didn’t feel the need for that. She decided to travel and see the world and learn what those hateful Dwarfs have done to it and stop their next attempt to try and take over another forest that doesn’t belong to them.
